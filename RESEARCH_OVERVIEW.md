# Research Overview

## Program

NOESIS is a long-term research program in computational epistemology. It studies how computational systems can preserve the distinction between observations, interpretations, decisions, authority, and outcomes while retaining enough provenance for later audit and reconstruction.

Polaris is the reference implementation and experimental testbed.

## Safety-relevant autonomous-agent track

A current empirical domain is autonomous AI development. The central question is not whether an agent can produce useful code, but whether useful autonomy can coexist with externally controlled consequence boundaries.

Current research question:

> Can increasingly autonomous AI development agents be granted useful operational authority while keeping authorization, evidence, evaluation, recovery, and replay externally verifiable and non-self-certifying across model and execution boundaries?

The system therefore treats a model as a proposer and bounded actor rather than a normative authority. Producing a plan, patch, candidate, or success statement does not by itself authorize execution or establish semantic success.

## Existing bounded evidence

Earlier operational work established a multi-host control substrate capable of bounded task admission, fail-closed rejection, disposable execution, interruption/recovery handling, and independently checked terminal outcomes under its tested conditions.

These are operational assurance results, not universal safety claims.

A completed frozen cross-model probe subsequently failed to transfer cleanly to a different model/runtime combination. Preserved evidence localized much of the failure to an unexpected response-surface/normalization dependency. The negative result was retained and used to motivate a stricter cross-model experiment rather than being retrospectively repaired.

## Current and proposed work

The current program strengthens the development-agent substrate through preregistered real development episodes, independent outcome boundaries, injected interruption, explicit authority ambiguity, deterministic Cold Replay, adversarial falsification cases, and preservation of failed and abandoned trajectories.

A separate cross-model independence experiment freezes the surrounding task and control contract while changing model identity. Additional independently developed model families can then be evaluated as replication arms without retroactively changing the primary preregistration.

## Research discipline

NOESIS distinguishes:

- established observations from planned tests;
- operational assurance from scientific generalization;
- model output from authorization;
- self-checks from independent outcome evidence;
- persistent identity from persistent privilege;
- absence of evidence from evidence of absence.

The intended output is bounded evidence about which control properties survive changes in model, environment, interruption, and execution topology—and which do not.
