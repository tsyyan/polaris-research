# Public Review Claims Boundary

Version: **public-review-claims-v1**

## Demonstrated public claims

The public files demonstrate only that:

- Polaris publishes an evidence-conservative conceptual methodology;
- the methodology distinguishes sealed reconstruction from later live
  observation;
- this package exposes a bounded Pilot-006 record and content identities for
  selected undisclosed artifacts; and
- the public reviewer workflow explicitly records unavailable evidence.

## Privately evidenced claims

Private canonical records report that Pilot-006 processed seven declared
sources, classified five as `DRIFT_OBSERVED` and two as `BYTE_IDENTICAL`, did
not substitute live bytes into sealed reconstruction, and matched deterministic
replay. These are reported in the public projection but cannot be independently
verified from this package.

## Methodological proposals

The suitability of content-bound identity, dual-track acquisition, fail-closed
gates, evidence-role separation, and bounded evaluation for other studies
remains open to review and future validation.

## Non-claims

Polaris does not claim incident truth, root cause, corpus completeness,
semantic equivalence from byte identity, universal provenance correctness,
generalization, human benefit, production readiness, production authority, or
complete reproduction from public materials.

## Unresolved limitations

- Private evidence and experiment records are unavailable publicly.
- Raw third-party source content is excluded.
- Public reviewers cannot inspect the full lineage or repeat deterministic
  replay.
- Host, transport, key-custody, evaluator, and external-source assumptions
  remain outside the demonstrated public scope.
- Controlled access is not currently offered.
